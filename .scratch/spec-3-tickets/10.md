## Parent

Part of #3 (Sales and reporting).

## What to build

Provide a stock-on-hand and low-stock report by store with product search, in the reporting module, with CSV export. Zero-stock products remain visible when they match the filter. This report reuses the inventory low-stock flag rather than redefining it, and adds search, store filtering, and Thai-safe CSV export.

`Covers:` RPT-02 (stock-on-hand and low-stock); RPT-04 (stock CSV). AC-08.
`Demo:` the B01 stock report lists SKU003 (balance 0) in the low-stock section because 0 is at or below its threshold of 3; CSV exports with a BOM
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; web component test

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A zero-stock product at or below its threshold remains visible and is flagged low-stock (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [AC-08]
- [ ] CSV export applies the same filters and permissions as the screen and is Thai-safe (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [RPT-04]
