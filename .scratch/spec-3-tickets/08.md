## Parent

Part of #3 (Sales and reporting).

## What to build

Build the shared reporting primitives every report reuses: a reporting-day boundary that runs from 00:00:00 inclusive to the next day 00:00:00 exclusive in Asia/Bangkok on a given timestamp, and a CSV-safety helper that neutralizes cells beginning with a formula trigger (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, and leading tab/CR) per OWASP guidance and encodes output as UTF-8 with a BOM so Thai text opens correctly. These are pure, unit-tested helpers.

`Covers:` RPT display rules (reporting-day boundary); RPT-04 (CSV safety primitives)
`Demo:` a sale confirmed at 23:30 and one at 00:30 Asia/Bangkok fall on different reporting days; a product name beginning with `=` is neutralized in CSV output
`Seams:` Zod/util spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The day-boundary helper places a 23:30 and a 00:30 Asia/Bangkok timestamp on different reporting days, with the window `[00:00, next 00:00)` (util spec; fails today — no helper).
- [ ] The CSV helper neutralizes a value beginning with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab, or CR (util spec; fails today).
- [ ] CSV output is encoded UTF-8 with a BOM and preserves Thai text (util spec; fails today).
