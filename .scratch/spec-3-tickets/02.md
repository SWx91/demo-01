## Parent

Part of #3 (Sales and reporting).

## What to build

Let a Store employee create a draft sale for their assigned store using active products. The current selling price is read from the product and captured onto the line; the user cannot override it. A draft does not reduce stock. Each line amount is quantity times the captured unit price, and the document total is the sum of line amounts, to two decimal places. Inactive products cannot be added (shared guard). Store scope comes from identity.

`Covers:` SAL-01 (active products, assigned store); SAL-02 (draft does not reduce stock); US-02; US-09
`Demo:` staff_b01 drafts a sale of SKU001; the price 60.00 is captured and cannot be overridden; an inactive product is refused; stock is unchanged
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; web component test
