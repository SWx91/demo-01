## What to build

Guarantee that two sales confirmed at the same time for the same product and store cannot oversell. The guarded conditional update serializes the deduction at the database, so exactly one of two competing confirmations succeeds and the final balance is correct.

`Covers:` INV-04 applied to sales. AC-07.
`Demo:` with SKU001 stock at 10, two concurrent sales of 7 each — exactly one succeeds, the other is rejected, and final stock is 3
`Seams:` Postgres integration spec
