## Parent

Part of #3 (Sales and reporting).

## What to build

Let a Store employee confirm a sale. In one database transaction, validate the latest stock for every line against the current persisted balance, allocate a sale number, write the sale lines, decrease the store's stock balance with a guarded update that cannot go negative, and write SALE stock movements with before/after balances through the shared movement mechanism. The sale is audited (actor, timestamp, source document, outcome). This is the first ticket that writes stock in this spec and reuses the Postgres integration harness.

`Covers:` SAL-03 (number, decrease, movement, one transaction); US-06. AC-06.
`Demo:` staff_b01 confirms a sale of 2 packs of SKU001 and 1 pack of SKU002 → document total THB 155.00, balances become 8 and 4
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; Postgres integration spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Confirming a sale allocates a sale number, decreases the store's balance, and writes SALE movements with before/after balances, all in one transaction (Postgres integration spec; fails today — no confirm route). [SAL-03]
- [ ] Selling 2 packs of SKU001 and 1 pack of SKU002 totals THB 155.00 and leaves balances 8 and 4 (oRPC controller spec + Postgres integration spec; fails today). [AC-06]
- [ ] A confirmed sale is recorded in an audit entry with actor, timestamp, source document, and outcome (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [NFR-03]
