## Parent

Part of #3 (Sales and reporting).

## What to build

Define the sale model and its contract: a `Sale` (number, store, actor, status, confirmedAt, total) with `SaleLine`s (product, quantity, unit price captured at confirmation, line amount). The contract's Zod schemas are the source of truth and reject non-positive or non-whole sale quantities. Monetary values use the two-decimal representation chosen in the inventory foundation, never float.

`Covers:` SAL-01 (quantity rules at the contract boundary)
`Demo:` the sale schema rejects a non-positive and a fractional sale quantity at the contract boundary
`Seams:` Zod schema spec; oRPC controller spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The sale schema carries number, store, status, total, and lines with product, quantity, captured unit price, and line amount, round-tripping through the contract (Zod schema spec; fails today — no sales contract).
- [ ] A non-positive sale quantity is rejected at the contract boundary (Zod schema spec; fails today).
- [ ] A fractional sale quantity is rejected at the contract boundary (Zod schema spec; fails today).
- [ ] Monetary fields preserve exactly two decimal places through a contract call (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
