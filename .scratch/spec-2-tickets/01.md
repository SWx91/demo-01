## Parent

Part of #2 (Purchasing and goods receiving).

## What to build

Define the purchase order model and its contract: a `PurchaseOrder` (supplier, destination store, expected receipt date, status, creator) with `PurchaseOrderLine`s (product, ordered quantity, purchase unit price), and the status enum DRAFT, PENDING_APPROVAL, APPROVED, PARTIALLY_RECEIVED, COMPLETED, REJECTED, CANCELLED. The contract's Zod schemas are the source of truth and reject invalid lines: ordered quantities must be positive whole numbers, purchase unit prices cannot be negative, no SKU may appear twice in one order, and an order needs at least one line.

`Covers:` PO-01 (fields); PO-02 (status enum)
`Demo:` the purchase-order schema rejects a negative price, a fractional ordered quantity, and a duplicate-SKU order at the contract boundary
`Seams:` Zod schema spec; oRPC controller spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The purchase-order schema carries supplier, destination store, expected receipt date, status, and at least one line with product, ordered quantity, and purchase unit price (Zod schema spec; fails today — no purchasing contract).
- [ ] A non-positive or non-whole ordered quantity is rejected at the contract boundary (Zod schema spec; fails today).
- [ ] A negative purchase unit price is rejected at the contract boundary (Zod schema spec; fails today).
- [ ] An order repeating a SKU across two lines is rejected at the contract boundary (Zod schema spec; fails today).
- [ ] The status enum exposes all seven states and round-trips through the contract (Zod schema spec; fails today).
