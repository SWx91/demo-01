## Parent

Part of #2 (Purchasing and goods receiving).

## What to build

Let the creator or a Manager cancel an order with a reason, but only while it is Draft or Pending Approval. Approved and Partially Received orders cannot be cancelled in this release.

`Covers:` PO-04 (cancel Draft/Pending with reason)
`Demo:` buyer01 cancels a draft with a reason; cancelling an Approved order is refused
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The creator or a Manager cancels a Draft or Pending Approval order with a reason; it becomes Cancelled (oRPC controller spec; fails today — no cancel route).
- [ ] Cancelling an Approved or Partially Received order is rejected by the status machine (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
- [ ] Cancellation without a reason is rejected (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
