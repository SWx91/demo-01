## Parent

Part of #2 (Purchasing and goods receiving).

## What to build

Enforce that a receipt cannot over-receive or add goods that were never ordered. Cumulative received quantity per line can never exceed the ordered quantity, and a product outside the order cannot be added to the receiving screen. A rejected receipt creates no receipt, no movement, and no stock change, and leaves the order status unchanged.

`Covers:` GR-02 (cumulative cap, no non-ordered product); GR-01. AC-03.
`Demo:` staff_b01 attempting to receive 21 against the 20-unit PO-DEMO-001 is rejected; no receipt or movement is created, stock stays 10, status stays Approved
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; Postgres integration spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A receipt whose cumulative received quantity would exceed the ordered quantity is rejected (oRPC controller spec + Postgres integration spec; fails today). [AC-03]
- [ ] Receiving 21 against a 20-unit order from the starting data creates no receipt or movement, stock stays 10, and status stays Approved (Postgres integration spec; fails today). [AC-03]
- [ ] A product not on the order cannot be received against it (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [GR-02]
