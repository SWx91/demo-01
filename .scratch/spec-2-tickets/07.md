## Parent

Part of #2 (Purchasing and goods receiving).

## What to build

Let a Store employee confirm a goods receipt against an Approved or Partially Received order for their store. In one database transaction, allocate a goods-receipt number, write the received lines, increase the destination store's stock balance, write RECEIPT stock movements with before/after balances through the shared movement mechanism, and recompute the order status to Partially Received if any quantity remains outstanding or Completed when every line is fully received. Each received quantity is a non-negative whole number and at least one line must be greater than zero. The receipt is audited (actor, timestamp, source document, outcome) and, once confirmed, is viewable but not editable or cancellable. This is the first ticket that writes stock in this spec and reuses the Postgres integration harness.

`Covers:` GR-02 (partial receipt, quantity validation); GR-03 (number, stock increase, status recompute); GR-05 (confirmed receipt immutable); US-18/19/24. AC-01.
`Demo:` staff_b01 receives 8 of 20 of SKU001 against PO-DEMO-001 → B01 stock 18, outstanding 12, Partially Received; receiving the remaining 12 → stock 30, Completed
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; Postgres integration spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Confirming a receipt allocates a goods-receipt number, increases the destination store's balance, and writes RECEIPT movements with before/after balances, all in one transaction (Postgres integration spec; fails today — no receiving). [AC-01]
- [ ] Receiving 8 of 20 of SKU001 against PO-DEMO-001 leaves B01 stock 18, outstanding 12, status Partially Received, with exactly one receipt and one movement (oRPC controller spec + Postgres integration spec; fails today). [AC-01]
- [ ] Receiving the remaining 12 leaves stock 30 and status Completed (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [AC-01]
- [ ] A receipt with all lines zero, or any negative/fractional received quantity, is rejected (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [GR-02]
- [ ] A confirmed receipt is viewable but cannot be edited or cancelled (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [GR-05]
- [ ] The receipt is recorded in an audit entry with actor, timestamp, source document, and outcome (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [NFR-03]
