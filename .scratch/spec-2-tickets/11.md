## Parent

Part of #2 (Purchasing and goods receiving).

## What to build

Allow receiving against an approved order even when the product was deactivated after approval, per the recorded Q-02 decision. A product deactivated after its order was approved may still be received against that approved order until the order closes; the Manager authorizes this as a recorded exception. The inactive-product guard blocks adding inactive products to new documents, but does not block receiving lines on an already-approved order.

`Covers:` Q-02 decision; GR-03 continuity
`Demo:` deactivate a product that is on an Approved order, then receive it successfully; the exception is recorded
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; Postgres integration spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A product deactivated after its order was approved can still be received against that approved order (oRPC controller spec + Postgres integration spec; fails today — guard would block it).
- [ ] The inactive-on-approved receipt is recorded as an authorized exception (actor/outcome) (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
- [ ] The same product still cannot be added to a brand-new order while inactive (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
