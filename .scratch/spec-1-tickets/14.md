## Parent

Part of #1 (Master data and inventory foundation).

## What to build

Let a Manager increase or decrease stock with a mandatory reason, writing an ADJUSTMENT movement through the same one-transaction mechanism. A reduction larger than the available balance is rejected. The adjustment is audited with actor, timestamp, source document, and outcome — this ticket establishes the audit-record convention the sibling specs reuse.

`Covers:` INV-05 (adjust with reason, reject over-reduction); US-17/US-18; NFR-03 (audit)
`Demo:` manager01 increases SKU002 by 5 with a reason; a reduction below zero is rejected
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; Postgres integration spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Manager adjusts stock up or down; the adjustment requires a non-empty reason and writes an ADJUSTMENT movement with before/after balances (oRPC controller spec; fails today — no adjustment route).
- [ ] A reduction greater than the available balance is rejected and no movement is written (oRPC controller spec + Postgres integration spec; fails today).
- [ ] A non-manager calling adjust directly is rejected with no data change (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [AC-04 shape]
- [ ] The adjustment is recorded in an audit entry with actor, timestamp, source document, and outcome (oRPC controller spec; fails today). [NFR-03]
