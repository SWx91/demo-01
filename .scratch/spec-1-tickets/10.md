## Parent

Part of #1 (Master data and inventory foundation).

## What to build

Introduce the stock model and the single mechanism every stock change in the product will go through. A stock balance exists per product per store. Every increase or decrease writes an immutable stock movement recording product, store, quantity change, balance before and after, movement type, source document, actor, and timestamp. Posting a document, updating the balance, and writing the movement happen in one database transaction — all of it commits or none of it does — and the database itself refuses to let a balance go negative or a concurrent update to be lost (guarded conditional update or version column). Seed opening balances for B01 (SKU001=10, SKU002=5, SKU003=0) and zero for B02. This is the first ticket that writes stock.

`Covers:` INV-02 (movement fields); INV-03 (atomic posting, non-negative); INV-04 (no lost updates); US-13 (immutable movements); US-14 (balance = opening + successful movements)
`Demo:` seed opening balances; a movement records before/after balances; a balance can never be driven below zero
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; Postgres integration spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A stock movement records product, store, quantity change, balance before and after, movement type, source document, actor, and timestamp (oRPC controller spec; fails today — no movement model).
- [ ] A confirmed movement cannot be edited or deleted (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
- [ ] Posting that changes a balance and writes a movement commits together or rolls back together (Postgres integration spec; fails today). [INV-03]
- [ ] The database rejects any change that would drive a balance below zero (Postgres integration spec; fails today). [INV-03]
- [ ] Two updates to the same product+store do not lose an update; the final balance reflects both or correctly rejects one (Postgres integration spec; fails today). [INV-04]
- [ ] Opening balances are seeded: B01 SKU001=10, SKU002=5, SKU003=0; all B02 zero (observable by reading balances; fails today).
