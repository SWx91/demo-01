## What to build

Guarantee that editing master data never rewrites history. A stock movement captures a transaction-time snapshot of the product details it recorded, so later changes to the product's name or price leave existing movements and documents faithful to what they recorded.

`Covers:` MD shared rule (historical fidelity); US-19
`Demo:` change SKU001's selling price; an existing movement still shows the details recorded at the time
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; Postgres integration spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A movement stores a transaction-time snapshot of the product details it recorded (oRPC controller spec; fails today — no snapshot).
- [ ] Editing a product's name or price does not alter the details recorded in any existing movement (Postgres integration spec; fails today).
- [ ] A historical search of an edited product still shows the original recorded details on past movements (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
