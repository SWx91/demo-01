Part of #1 (Master data and inventory foundation).

## What to build

Define the product model and its contract: SKU, name, category, unit, selling price, and active status. The contract's Zod schemas are the source of truth and reject invalid master data — a negative selling price is refused, and quantities are constrained to whole numbers. Pick the two-decimal money representation that avoids floating-point drift (integer minor units or Decimal) and use it for the selling price. Per-store reorder threshold comes in a later ticket.

`Covers:` MD-01 (product fields); MD-02 (negative price rejected, whole-number quantities)
`Demo:` the product schema rejects a negative selling price and a fractional quantity at the contract boundary
`Seams:` Zod schema spec; oRPC controller spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The product schema carries SKU, name, category, unit, selling price, and active status, round-tripping through the contract (Zod schema spec; fails today — no product contract).
- [ ] A negative selling price is rejected at the contract boundary (Zod schema spec; fails today).
- [ ] A non-whole quantity is rejected at the contract boundary (Zod schema spec; fails today).
- [ ] The selling price uses the chosen two-decimal representation and preserves exactly two decimal places through a contract call (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
