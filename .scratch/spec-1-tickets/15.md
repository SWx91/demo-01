## What to build

Freeze a product's SKU once any transaction references it. Before any movement exists, the SKU can still be corrected; once a stock movement (or any document) references the product, attempts to change its SKU are rejected while other fields remain editable.

`Covers:` MD-01 (SKU frozen after a transaction exists); US-03
`Demo:` after a movement on SKU001, editing its SKU is rejected; editing its name still works
`Seams:` oRPC controller spec; Postgres integration spec

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A product with no referencing movement can still have its SKU corrected (oRPC controller spec; fails today — no immutability rule).
- [ ] Once a movement references the product, changing its SKU is rejected with a Thai actionable error (oRPC controller spec + Postgres integration spec; fails today).
- [ ] Other product fields remain editable after the SKU is frozen (oRPC controller spec; fails today).
